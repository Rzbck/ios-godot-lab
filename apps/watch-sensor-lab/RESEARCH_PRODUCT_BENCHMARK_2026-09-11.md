# Watch Sensor Lab — product benchmark / intelligence roadmap

Date: 2026-09-11

Purpose: retain the external product/science research used to evolve Watch Tracker into a compact, premium, depth-first sport + wellness application without inventing opaque health claims.

This document is research/product direction. It is not a hardware-validation record and does not replace the active HANDOFF.

## Core product rule

The app should not win by displaying the largest number of proprietary scores. It should make a small number of important questions immediately understandable, then let the user tap into evidence, provenance, confidence, and history.

Surface hierarchy:
1. Today / what matters now.
2. Daily Brief / what changed relative to personal history.
3. Activity / live sport context.
4. Progression / current-vs-previous and long-term trend.
5. Recovery / sleep / nocturnal vitals / cardio.
6. Load / volume / perceived and estimated effort.
7. Journal / correlations after sufficient observations.
8. History / source-aware drill-down.

Watch hierarchy remains horizontal by major function, with vertical/Crown/tap depth inside each function.

## Apple platform findings

### HealthKit

Use HealthKit as source-of-truth for readable system health/workout data, retaining source/date and never assuming authorization simply because a query returns no samples.

Important readable/usable signals already used or planned:
- workouts + routes;
- heart rate and resting heart rate;
- HRV SDNN;
- respiratory rate;
- sleep analysis/stages;
- sleeping wrist temperature;
- oxygen saturation where available;
- VO2 max;
- one-minute heart-rate recovery;
- body mass / height / date of birth;
- workout effort score and estimated workout effort on supported OS versions.

Current 2026 Apple developer documentation does not expose the Apple Sleep Score through HealthKit. Apple DTS publicly stated that there is no Sleep Score access API. Therefore Watch Tracker must not attempt to show “Apple Sleep Score” or pretend its own score is identical.

### Apple Sleep Score

Current Apple product behavior uses an official sleep score, but third-party API access is unavailable. Tracker direction:
- show sleep dimensions separately;
- compare duration to personal baseline;
- show consistency and continuity;
- show phases as source data, not universal targets;
- show nocturnal vitals separately;
- keep Tracker Recovery multi-signal and clearly distinct from Apple Sleep Score.

### Apple Vitals direction

Apple’s Vitals product emphasizes overnight measurements and personal typical ranges rather than only population thresholds. Tracker should apply the same useful design principle without cloning Apple UI:
- personal rolling baseline;
- deviation from own range;
- confidence / sample coverage;
- explain which signals are missing.

### Training load

Apple training load compares recent 7-day intensity/duration context against a longer 28-day reference. Tracker already uses 7d vs previous-28d weekly volume context, but should preserve separate components rather than claim a universal readiness formula.

### WorkoutKit

WorkoutKit supports creating/previewing/syncing structured workout compositions to Apple Workout, including:
- custom interval workouts;
- single-goal workouts;
- pacer workouts;
- SwimBikeRun / triathlon plans.

Future module candidate: “Plans”. It should be evaluated separately from the existing Watch-authoritative recorder. Do not replace current tracking authority merely to use WorkoutKit.

### Hypertension notifications

From iOS 26.2, Apple documents read access to `HKCategoryTypeIdentifierHypertensionEvent` for notifications generated by Apple’s Hypertension Notifications feature.

If integrated:
- keep it under Health / medical-context information, not sport readiness;
- use availability guards;
- never convert it to points in a recovery/fitness score;
- provide clear source and safety language.

## Benchmark: Garmin

Training Readiness combines multiple factors such as:
- last-night sleep;
- recovery time;
- HRV status;
- acute load;
- recent sleep history;
- recent stress history.

Useful pattern to borrow:
- several independent factors explain the readiness view;
- historical context matters more than one isolated sample.

Do not borrow:
- an opaque daily number without provenance;
- claims that exceed data available from Apple Watch / HealthKit.

## Benchmark: Strava

Current useful patterns:
- Relative Effort can use HR or perceived exertion and is personalized to HR zones;
- Fitness & Freshness separates fitness, fatigue, and form over time;
- long-term trend is more meaningful than a single absolute number;
- athlete-specific fitting can bridge different available training signals after enough paired samples;
- matched activities compare performances on repeated routes;
- Grade Adjusted Pace accounts for slope rather than comparing raw pace blindly;
- Athlete Intelligence summarizes an activity against relevant prior history.

Tracker opportunities:
- long-term fitness/fatigue/form view only after robust load data exists;
- match similar activities/routes automatically;
- compare pace/speed after accounting for elevation/context;
- activity-specific post-workout brief with provenance.

Do not build a fitness/fatigue model from a handful of sessions. Require meaningful history and show model coverage.

## Benchmark: WHOOP

Useful patterns:
- Recovery integrates multiple overnight signals;
- Journal captures behaviors without directly modifying the score;
- behavior impacts require repeated yes/no observations;
- WHOOP currently describes at least 5 “yes” and 5 “no” entries within 90 days before behavior impact analysis;
- insights are correlations/associations, not proof of causation;
- long-term Healthspan uses longer history and multiple independent health contributors.

Tracker direction already started:
- local behavior experiment journal;
- no tag directly changes Recovery;
- future behavior impact requires minimum data on both sides;
- show sample count and coverage;
- use association language only.

Do not build a “biological age” or “pace of aging” clone without validated methodology and the required data. Age/weight/VO2/RHR can be contextual inputs, not a fabricated age output.

## Benchmark: Oura

Useful patterns:
- Trends compare daily/weekly/monthly time ranges;
- web trend tools can overlay two metrics;
- Discovery Hub surfaces correlations only after a baseline period;
- current documentation states at least 14 of the previous 30 days are needed to establish baseline for Discoveries;
- tags do not directly change readiness/sleep/activity scores;
- Cardiovascular Age uses estimated pulse-wave velocity from PPG waveform, not VO2 alone.

Tracker direction:
- metric overlay/correlation views with `n` and confidence;
- no cardiovascular-age feature without an equivalent validated waveform/PWV measurement;
- keep tags/journal independent from scores;
- delay discoveries until adequate samples exist.

### Correlation Lab guardrails

Initial supported comparisons:
- sleep duration vs next-day HRV;
- sleep duration vs next-day resting HR;
- previous-day workout minutes vs following sleep duration.

Rules:
- display raw pair count `n`;
- no interpretation below minimum 10 pairs;
- show Pearson `r` only as an association metric;
- never state cause/effect;
- current first version uses the recent sleep window retained by Recovery; extend to longer history only after UX/validation.

### Behavior Journal guardrails

Initial local tags include evening, recovery, habits and contextual factors such as late caffeine, alcohol, late meal, late screen, meditation, sauna, mobility, hydration, nap, perceived high stress and travel.

Rules:
- local storage only in current implementation;
- tags do not write to Apple Health;
- tags do not change recovery/load scores;
- collect first, analyze later;
- yes/no behavior impact must require observations in both groups;
- notes are contextual and not automatically interpreted as medical facts.

## Physiology rules

### Age

Use Health date of birth only when authorized/readable. Age can contextualize adult HRmax fallback and age-adjusted presentation. Never directly penalize recovery merely because a person is older.

### Weight

Use current Health body-mass samples with source/date. Weight can contextualize calculations that mathematically require mass. Apple VO2 max is already expressed in mL/kg/min, so do not divide or multiply by body mass again.

### HRmax

Reference hierarchy:
1. user/personal configured maximum;
2. measured reliable personal context when deliberately supported;
3. adult age estimate `208 - 0.7 × age` as fallback with low-confidence label;
4. session peak only as last-resort session context.

Do not imply an age formula is the user’s true physiological HRmax.

### VO2 max

Keep Apple Health value, date and source. Use trend/personal history before population judgments. Do not derive “cardiovascular age” from VO2 max alone.

## Next product modules worth pursuing

### Near term

1. Validate Daily Brief / Sleep Lab / Journal / Correlation Lab on-device.
2. Expand Recovery history so correlations can use 30/60/90-day windows rather than only recent 14-night data.
3. Behavior-impact engine only after enough journal history exists.
4. Add matched-activity comparison:
   - same sport;
   - similar distance/duration;
   - optionally route fingerprint;
   - compare pace, HR, effort, weather, elevation.
5. Fitness / Fatigue / Form research implementation using transparent impulse-response model only after load history is sufficient.
6. sport-specific cards:
   - run: pace, grade-adjusted pace candidate, cadence, splits;
   - cycle: speed/cadence/power when actually available;
   - hike: ascent/descent, grade, route quality;
   - swim: distance/laps/stroke metrics only when supported by collected data;
   - strength: duration/RPE/sets only when explicitly captured.

### Mapping / terrain

Need a trustworthy surface source before road/trail/gravel labels. MapKit currently provides map presentation and routing context but should not be treated as an authoritative surface-type database for our recorded route.

Future terrain module should consider:
- route matching/map data source;
- surface type and confidence;
- slope distribution;
- elevation gain/loss;
- route difficulty;
- weather exposure;
- repeated-route comparison.

### Structured training

Evaluate a separate Plans module using WorkoutKit:
- interval builder;
- distance/time goals;
- pace alerts;
- pacer plans;
- triathlon plans;
- sync/open in Apple Workout where beneficial.

Keep this separate from active recorder authority until hardware validation proves the interaction model.

## UI/UX system

### iPhone

Primary principle: one dominant question per surface.
- Today = “What matters today?”
- Activity = “What is happening now?”
- Progression = “How am I changing?”
- Recovery = “Why do I feel/perform this way relative to my history?”
- History = “What happened in this session?”

Use:
- direct visual comparison;
- overlaid current/previous trajectories;
- small number of colored semantic accents;
- tap-to-depth;
- source/confidence close to analytical results;
- empty states that explain what data is missing.

Avoid:
- walls of KPI cards;
- percentage deltas without visual context;
- one universal color for every metric;
- invented zeros;
- medical-looking red alerts for normal sport variation.

### Apple Watch

Top-level major functions remain horizontal. Inside a major function, use vertical/Crown/tap depth and single-screen-height pages where possible.

Watch should answer quickly:
- Can I start?
- What sport?
- What is my key metric now?
- What is my recovery/status at a glance?
- What did I do recently?

Detailed multi-series charts remain primarily iPhone content.

## Validation boundaries

CI validates compilation/packaging only.

Still require real-device verification for:
- Health authorization and actual readable fields;
- sleep/vitals values and layout;
- Watch wellness sync;
- different Watch case sizes;
- active workout interaction/haptics;
- Fitness/Forme source name/type/icon;
- background/lock behavior;
- battery/long-session stability;
- disconnect/reconnect and duplicate prevention.

No feature should be called physically validated until the user observes it on the actual iPhone/Watch.
